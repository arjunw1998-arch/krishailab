# Facts — UPI
- UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is run by NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India); launched 2016 (pilot April, public August 2016). Source: NPCI.
- Flow (simplified): payer's UPI app → NPCI switch → payer's bank verifies the UPI PIN and balance and debits → NPCI → payee's bank credits → both sides notified. Typically completes in a few seconds.
- Volume: UPI crossed 20 billion transactions in a single month in August 2025 (NPCI monthly statistics); "20 अरब से ज़्यादा हर महीने" is true from then on. Re-check NPCI's latest month before upload.
- Cost: most person-to-person and person-to-merchant UPI payments carry no fee for the user ("ज़्यादातर फ़्री").
