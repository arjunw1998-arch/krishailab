# Facts — Chandrayaan-3
- Launched 14 July 2023 from Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota, on LVM3 (ISRO).
- About 40 days: Earth-bound orbit raising, trans-lunar injection, lunar orbit reduction.
- Vikram lander touched down on 23 August 2023 at 18:04 IST near the lunar south pole.
- India: first country to land near the lunar south pole; fourth to soft-land on the Moon (USSR 1966 Luna 9, USA 1966 Surveyor 1, China 2013 Chang'e 3, India 2023).
- Pragyan rover's LIBS instrument confirmed sulphur in the south polar surface (ISRO, 29 Aug 2023).
- Cost about ₹615 crore (widely reported figure for the mission).
- 23 August declared National Space Day by the Government of India (first observed 2024).
- "Cheaper than many Hollywood films" — e.g. Interstellar's reported budget (~US$165 million) exceeds ₹615 crore (~US$75 million).
