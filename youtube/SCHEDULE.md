# KrishAILab: daily upload schedule

**Rule: one Short a day for the first 30 days. Same time, every day.**
A new channel grows through consistency and data. Every Short is a test, and posting daily gives YouTube 30 tests to learn your audience from in month one.

## Posting times (IST)
| Day | Time | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Fri | **7:30 PM** | Indian evening peak, after work and college |
| Sat–Sun | **11:00 AM** | Weekend late-morning scrolling |

Schedule every upload in YouTube Studio (*Schedule* rather than *Publish*), so a busy day never breaks the streak.

## Your 15-minute daily routine
1. **Before posting:** paste the kit (title, description, tags, pinned comment), upload the `.srt`, set the cover frame, add to the playlist, set the *Related video*.
2. **First 10 minutes:** share the link to your WhatsApp and Instagram story; pin the comment.
3. **First hour:** reply to every comment (replies count as engagement and bring people back).
4. **Next day:** check *Viewed vs. swiped away* in Studio:
   - **above 70 % viewed:** the hook works, so make more like it;
   - **below 50 %:** the first 2 seconds lost people, so open the next one faster (start on the question, not the intro).
5. **Every Sunday:** list the 2 best and 2 worst by average % viewed. Next week, make more of the best topic and style.

Don't change titles in the first 48 h, don't delete low performers (Shorts often pick up after days), and don't repost the same video.

## What to look for after 30 days
- **Which language** gets more returning viewers (Studio → Audience). Double down on it, but keep the other at 2 a week.
- **Which style** holds best (average % viewed). Keep 2–3 signature looks rather than all of them, so the channel stays recognisable.
- **Subscribers per 1,000 views.** Above 5 means the series format is working. Below 2 means the end card needs a stronger reason to follow ("Part 2 tomorrow").

No schedule guarantees virality. Daily posting maximises the number of chances and the data you learn from.
